French Lawn

Experience the romance of a sophisticated, mid-sized garden oasis. Defined by neat geometric layouts, manicured hedges, and elegant paved pathways, the French Lawn offers a refined European charm for couples seeking a stylish, upscale setting.

Perfect for mid-sized wedding ceremonies, vibrant Mehendi functions, and open-air cocktail receptions. The structured landscape naturally frames your layout, providing a picturesque backdrop that requires very little extra decor.

  • 50 to 300 guests — ideal for mid-sized celebrations
  • Symmetrical, European-inspired garden design
  • Paved walkways for a grand bridal entry
  • Integrated ambient lighting for evening events
