In and Around

Discover the Nearby Hidden Wonders

Water Falls

Kovai Kutralam is a scenic spot with a gentle waterfall originating on the Siruvani hill ranges. It is located on the western ghat mountain range that lies to the west of this city at a distance of about 23 km from CSR Landmark Resorts
Isha Foundation

Isha Yoga Centre / Isha Foundation — about 21 km (approx. 20–30 minutes via Siruvani Main Rd). A serene spiritual destination featuring Adiyogi and Dhyanalinga, ideal for guests staying at CSR Landmark Resorts.
Temples

Arulmigu Subramaniya Swamy Temple, Marudhamalai is a hilltop Hindu temple (20 km): This is a beautiful ancient Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Murugan.
Temples

Arulmigu Patteeswarar Swamy Temple (14 km): This is a beautiful ancient Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Shiva.

Waterfall

Monkey Falls are natural waterfalls located near the uphill ghat road Valparai on the Pollachi-Valparai road in the Anaimalai Hills range, in Coimbatore district. Monkey Falls is about 80 km from CSR Landmark Resorts.

Temple
