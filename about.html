Our History

CSR Landmark Resorts, located amidst 132 acres of lush mango farms, offers a luxurious retreat with a fusion of modern French and Balinese charm. From the whispering winds through the trees to the gentle rustle of leaves, every detail of the resort embodies a symphony of serenity. We believe that every guest deserves a unique and unforgettable experience.

For those seeking an elevated experience, we offer the option to upgrade to our spacious suites featuring separate living areas, dining rooms, and balconies, providing added space and luxury during your getaway. We pride ourselves on our commitment to sustainability, sourcing the best ingredients from farmers to create world-class cuisine in our multicuisine restaurant .

Our world-class amenities and convenient services like free and high-speed Wi-Fi, private parking, and welcome drinks make us a premier destination for hosting memorable event spaces and events. We craft experiences that last long in your memories even after your departure.

Step into wonder and awaken your spirits with CSR Landmark Resorts !
