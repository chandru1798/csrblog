Activities

Provided with the Different venues in CSR LANDMARK RESORTS offers you a memorable experience for your Event commingled with the Nature & Hills surrounded.

Go-Karting: Looking for adventure? Experience the thrill of Go-Karting at CSR Landmark Resorts — an exciting on-site activity perfect for families, friends, and corporate day-outs.

Swimming Pool:You can enjoy a refreshing swim in our swimming pool, but don't forget to wear your swimwear nylon dress.

Kids Play Area:For children, we have a Kids Play Area, where adventure awaits around every corner. With interactive games and safe surroundings, it's the perfect spot for endless fun and laughter.

Indoor Games:For indoor enthusiasts, our facilities offer a range of games including Table Tennis, Carrom, Chess, Archery, Foos Ball, Tether Ball, and Othello.

Outdoor Games:Outdoors, challenge friends and family to cricket, try your hand at the bowling machine, or engage in a friendly match of badminton, volleyball, or football.

Pet Area:Introduce your children to the wonders of nature at our Kids Pet Area, where they can interact with friendly ducks.

Village Walk & Forest Walk:For those seeking a deeper connection with nature, join our guided treks through the village and forest trails, spanning approximately 5 kilometers to the foothills.
