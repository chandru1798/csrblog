Cost of a Resort Wedding in Coimbatore: 2026 Budget Guide

Planning a resort wedding in Coimbatore involves balancing your vision with a realistic budget. Costs vary based on guest count, venue package, catering, decor, and the number of events. This guide gives you a broad idea of what to expect so you can plan your celebration with confidence.

Average Cost Ranges

Resort weddings in Coimbatore can range from moderate to premium. Venue Rental and Packages

Venue costs typically cover the space for your events (lawn, hall, poolside, etc.) and may include basic facilities. Some resorts offer all-inclusive packages that bundle accommodation, food, and event space. Clarify what is includedâ€”guest rooms, setup, cleanup, and any time limitsâ€”before finalizing.

Catering

Catering is often one of the largest expenses. Costs depend on menu type (vegetarian, non-vegetarian, multiple cuisines), service style (buffet vs. plated), and guest count. In-house resort catering can simplify logistics; external caterers may be allowed with a fee. Plan a tasting and get a per-plate or per-person estimate.

Decor and Styling

From mandap and stage design to floral arrangements and lighting, decor can be tailored to your budget. Discuss your vision with decorators and ask for itemized quotes. Many venues, including CSR Landmark, have preferred or recommended vendors who know the space well.

Accommodation and Guest Stay

If your guests are staying at the resort, room blocks and group rates can reduce per-night costs. Factor in welcome bags, breakfast, or other hospitality if you plan to offer them.

Entertainment and Other Costs

Music, DJ, photography, videography, and gifts add to the total. Prioritize what matters most and get quotes early so you can adjust other elements if needed.

Tips to Stay Within Budget

Finalize your guest list early, choose a venue that fits your scale, and lock in key vendors with clear contracts. Consider hosting fewer events or simplifying decor if you need to trim costs. Our team at CSR Landmark can help you explore options that match your budget.

Final Thoughts

The cost of a resort wedding in Coimbatore depends on your choices and scale. With clear planning and the right venue partner, you can create a memorable celebration without overspending. Contact CSR Landmark Resort for current packages and pricing for your wedding.
